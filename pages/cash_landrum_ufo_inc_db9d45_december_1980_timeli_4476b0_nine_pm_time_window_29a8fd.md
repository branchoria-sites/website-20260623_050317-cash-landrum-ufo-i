## Introduction

Can the commonly cited 9 p.m. [timeline]({{ 'timeline/' | relative_url }}) of the Cash–Landrum incident actually fit the sequence of events described by the witnesses? Based on the witnesses’ own later statements, the answer is broadly yes, but only if several estimates are accepted as approximate rather than precise. The reported chronology depends on three linked time anchors: an evening meal near the junction of US 59 and FM 1485, a drive of roughly twelve miles before the sighting, and [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }})’s later claim that she arrived home around 9:50 p.m. When those elements are placed together, they create a relatively narrow encounter window between about 9:00 and 9:30 p.m. rather than an open-ended evening mystery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

## Dinner and Departure Estimates

The starting point for most timeline reconstructions is the group's meal stop after their unsuccessful search for bingo games. In later accounts, Betty Cash stated that they ate at a truck stop restaurant and placed the meal at roughly 8:00 to 8:30 p.m. The exact minute was never firmly established, but the estimate consistently places them back on the road before the alleged encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

That estimate matters because the route was not especially long. According to Cash's later statements, the sighting occurred after they had travelled about twelve miles from the restaurant area. Under ordinary night-driving conditions, twelve miles on rural roads would typically require only a modest amount of time. Even allowing for slower speeds on winding roads, the journey could plausibly place the witnesses near the reported encounter [location]({{ 'location/' | relative_url }}) sometime around 9 p.m. if they departed shortly after eating.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash%E2%80%93Landrum_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cash–Landrum incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cash–Landrum incident</span></span></span>

The timeline therefore does not require an unusually long unexplained gap between dinner and the first reported observation. The basic travel distance and the claimed departure time can be made to align without obvious contradiction.

## Does the Twelve-Mile Drive Leave Enough Time?

The most significant timing issue is not the drive to the encounter but the length of the encounter itself.

The witnesses did not describe a brief flash in the sky. They reported stopping the car, observing [the object]({{ 'the-object/' | relative_url }}) at close range, experiencing intense heat, returning to the vehicle, and then continuing their journey while allegedly seeing helicopters and the object again. Later summaries of the case frequently cite Betty Cash's estimate that the close encounter phase lasted roughly fifteen to seventeen minutes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

If that estimate is accepted, a simplified reconstruction looks something like this:

* Dinner concludes around 8:00–8:30 p.m.
* The group drives approximately twelve miles.
* The unusual light is noticed and develops into the reported close encounter.
* The stop on the road lasts roughly fifteen to seventeen minutes.
* The witnesses resume driving and continue towards Dayton.
* Betty Cash later reports arriving home at approximately 9:50 p.m.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

Nothing in that sequence obviously exceeds the available time. In fact, the reported stop is one reason many researchers settle on a sighting window somewhere between about 9:00 and 9:30 p.m. Rather than a single fixed moment, the event occupies a block of time within that half-hour period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

The challenge is that every element is estimated. The witnesses were not consulting clocks during the experience, and no independently verified timestamp exists for the roadside stop. As a result, the reconstruction works as a plausible timeline rather than a documented one.

## Arrival Home as the Backward Time Anchor

Among all the timing claims, the reported arrival home is arguably the most useful anchor because it places a limit on how late the encounter could have occurred.

Cash later stated that she reached home at approximately 9:50 p.m. Investigators and writers have often worked backwards from that figure. If the encounter happened substantially later than 9:30 p.m., there would be less room for the remaining drive and for the continuing helicopter observations described by the witnesses. Conversely, if the encounter began much earlier than 9 p.m., the evening would contain a larger unexplained gap between dinner and arrival.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

This is why the 9:00–9:30 estimate became so influential. It is not derived from a single recorded observation. Instead, it emerges from fitting together the witnesses' own reported milestones:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Time of the meal.
* Distance driven after leaving the restaurant.
* Claimed duration of exposure.
* Reported arrival home.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/801229huffman_dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[nicap.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportBetty Cash and Vicki &amp; Colby Landrum suffered apparent radiation illness after watching flame-spewing UFO and mystery helicopte...</span></span></span>

</div>

When those pieces are assembled, the encounter window naturally settles into the first half of the 9 o'clock hour.

## Where the Reconstruction Remains Uncertain

The strongest limitation of the timeline is that every major timestamp originates from recollection rather than contemporaneous documentation. No restaurant receipt has become a standard reference point in the case literature. No police report fixed the sighting to a precise minute.
