## Introduction

The 17 August 1981 meeting at [Bergstrom Air Force]({{ 'bergstrom/' | relative_url }}) Base was not a UFO investigation in the traditional sense. It was a claims interview conducted by Air Force legal personnel who were trying to determine whether the witnesses' story could be connected to an identifiable government activity. In practical terms, the session became an evidence hunt focused on one question: could the reported helicopters be traced to a military organisation and therefore form the basis of a claim against the US government? The transcript shows officials repeatedly steering the discussion toward details that might be documented later—numbers of helicopters, markings, rotor configurations, flight directions and witness certainty—while testing how much of the account rested on direct observation and how much rested on assumption. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. The officials were not merely collecting a narrative; they were looking for facts that could survive verification.

## Who Attended the August 1981 Meeting

The interview took place at Bergstrom Air Force Base on 17 August 1981. According to the transcript, the Air Force side consisted of Captain John Camp, Acting Staff Judge Advocate; Captain Terry Davis, Claims Officer; and Pat Wolf, Assistant Claims Officer. [Betty Cash]({{ 'betty-cash/' | relative_url }}), [Vickie Landrum]({{ 'vickie-landrum/' | relative_url }}) and Colby Landrum attended as witnesses. The session was recorded and later transcribed. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon+2NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. The composition of the meeting matters. None of the participants represented a dedicated UFO-investigation office. Instead, they came from the legal and claims structure that would handle allegations of government responsibility. This helps explain the style of questioning. Rather than asking speculative questions about unidentified objects, the interviewers concentrated on details that might identify a military aircraft, a unit, a base or a chain of command. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. The transcript also records how the case arrived at Bergstrom. The witnesses had contacted elected officials, including Texas senators, and were directed toward the Judge Advocate claims process. ## Why the Questioning Focused on Helicopters

For a claims office, the helicopters were the most useful part of the story. An unusual airborne object might be difficult to identify, but military helicopters would normally leave an administrative trail. If the witnesses could reliably describe aircraft types, markings or operational details, investigators could search records and potentially connect the event to a government organisation. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. Part 1 of 2. TRANSCRIPT. of taped interview...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 1981</span></span></span>

The interviewers therefore spent considerable time examining the helicopter observations. They asked how many aircraft were present, whether the witnesses had seen one or two rotors, how close the helicopters were, what markings were visible and whether the witnesses could identify specific types. The questioning repeatedly returned to observation rather than interpretation. Officials wanted to know exactly what had been seen rather than what witnesses believed the aircraft represented. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. Part 1 of 2. TRANSCRIPT. of taped interview...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 1981</span></span></span>

This emphasis reflected the realities of proving a government claim. A report that twenty or more military helicopters accompanied an object near a major population centre would, in theory, offer numerous opportunities for verification through flight operations, maintenance records, radar data, air-traffic reporting or unit logs. The helicopter component was therefore the bridge between personal testimony and documentary evidence. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.blueblurrylines.com/2019/03/the-us-governments-cash-landrum-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blueblurrylines.com">[Blue Blurry Lines]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blueblurrylines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the us governments cash landrum ufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Blue Blurry LinesThe US Government&#x27;s Cash-Landrum UFO Investigations7 Mar 2019 — They were interviewed by Captain John Camp, Acting Staff...</span></span></span>

## What the Transcript Shows About Uncertainty and Proof

One of the most revealing aspects of the Bergstrom interview is how often the discussion turns to certainty. The officials repeatedly asked witnesses to distinguish between what they definitely saw and what they inferred. This is characteristic of claims investigations, where confidence levels matter as much as the underlying account. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. Part 1 of 2. TRANSCRIPT. of taped interview...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 1981</span></span></span>

The transcript shows interviewers probing specific points:

* Whether aircraft markings were actually read or merely assumed.
* Whether the witnesses could confidently identify helicopter models.
* Whether estimates of helicopter numbers were counts or impressions.
* Whether [the object]({{ 'the-object/' | relative_url }})'s shape, engines or structure could truly be observed through the intense light and glare described by the witnesses. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cufon.org/cufon/cashlani.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufon.org">[Cufon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bergstrom AFB Interview of Betty Cash, Vickie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANSCRIPT OF BERGSTROM AFB INTERVIEW OF BETTY CASH, VICKIE &amp; COLBY LANDRUM August 1981. Part 1 of 2. TRANSCRIPT. of taped interview...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 1981</span></span></span>

The questioning often narrowed broad claims into evidential fragments.
